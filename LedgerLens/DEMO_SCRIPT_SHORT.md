# LedgerLens — 3-minute demo script

Short cut of `DEMO_SCRIPT.md`: same setup checklist (do the off-camera API-key run first), same files. About 340 spoken words — roughly 2¼ minutes of talking, which leaves time for clicks, page loads and short pauses inside 3 minutes. Web app first, then two terminal commands.

| # | Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 – 0:15 | App open on **Ingest**. | "This is LedgerLens, a bank statement analyzer for credit underwriting. It extracts every transaction from a statement, classifies it, checks the data, and gives a risk score with a decision." |
| 2 | 0:15 – 0:40 | Drag in **`02_multi_month_multi_account.pdf`**. Point at the pipeline turning green, then the **Data validation** panel. | "This file has three months and three accounts on twelve pages. Page order is checked, pages are read three at a time, and missing account details are inherited from earlier pages. Then twenty-two checks run — the key one is balance arithmetic on every row, which catches OCR misreads and edited PDFs." |
| 3 | 0:40 – 1:05 | Click **Transactions**, filter **EDUCATION**; then click **Review queue**. | "Every row gets a counterparty, channel, category, confidence and method — rule, LLM or manual. 'Tuition fee' becomes EDUCATION even when paid to a trust, and refunds and transfers from friends never count as income. Low-confidence rows go to review, but only the five that could change the decision." |
| 4 | 1:05 – 1:35 | Click **Risk**. Point at 777, then the decision and the override note, then the reasons. | "Six weighted components give 777 — 'Good', which on the score alone means approve. But hard rules win: structured cash deposits, money cycling with one party, and a bounced EMI, so the decision is REFER, and the screen says it's an override." |
| 5 | 1:35 – 2:00 | Drag in **`06_weak_applicant_3_months.pdf`** → **Risk**; then **`07_good_applicant_3_months.pdf`** → **Risk**. | "A weak applicant: EMIs take 75% of salary, two bounced, fifteen overdrawn days — DECLINE at 532. The same customer as before without the red flags: 931, APPROVE. A single month of data is never approved — under three months is referred." |
| 6 | 2:00 – 2:30 | Terminal: `node cli.js ../test-statements/05_amount_drcr_column.csv -o out.json` (API key set). Point at the `LLM extract` / `LLM classify` lines. | "The same engine runs from the command line, with Claude. This layout — one amount column with a Dr/Cr marker — defeats the rules, so the batch goes to Claude. Its rows come back as fixed JSON and pass through the same balance checks, uncertain rows get labels only from our taxonomy, and personal data is masked before anything is sent." *(Read the real row count from your screen.)* |
| 7 | 2:30 – 2:50 | `node test/eval-external.js --llm off` | "I also tested on statements from an independent project with answer keys. One layout fooled the rules — and at first, that wrong data passed every check. Now unreadable balances fail validation and send the page to the model." |
| 8 | 2:50 – 3:00 | Stop on the table. | "Rules first, the model for what rules can't settle, people only where it matters — and nothing scored that can't be verified. Thanks." |
